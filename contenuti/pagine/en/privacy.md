Nota Bene does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, forms, analytics or ads.

## Cookies and browser storage

The site uses no cookies and no `localStorage`. The only thing it saves in your browser is the piano samples used by the starting pitch and the playback check: it keeps them in the browser cache (`CacheStorage`) so they don't download every time. They are the site's own audio files, the same for everyone, and say nothing about who listens to them. You can delete them by clearing the site's data in your browser settings. That is why there is no banner to accept. The language is part of the page address, in `/it/` or `/en/`.

## What GitHub logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as it does for every GitHub Pages site. GitHub handles this data as an independent controller. The details are in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Fonts, libraries and sounds

Fonts, libraries and audio samples are served by the site itself, not by third-party services.

## Links to other sites

Links to CPDL and Mutopia, the sources of the pieces, lead to third-party sites with their own privacy rules. The same goes for other external links.

Emails sent to the Nota Bene address are used only to reply. The address is on the [contact](../contatti/) page.
