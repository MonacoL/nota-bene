Nota Bene does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, forms, analytics or ads.

## Cookies and browser storage

The site uses no cookies and saves nothing in your browser: no `localStorage` and no other storage. That is why there is no banner to accept. The language is part of the page address, in `/it/` or `/en/`.

## What GitHub logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as it does for every GitHub Pages site. GitHub handles this data as an independent controller: whoever runs Nota Bene does not see these logs and does not decide how they are used. The details are in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Fonts, libraries and sounds

Fonts, libraries and audio samples are served by the site itself, not by third-party services.

## Links to other sites

Links to CPDL and Mutopia, the sources of the pieces, lead to third-party sites with their own privacy rules. The same goes for other external links.

## If you write to us

Emails sent to the Nota Bene address are used only to reply. The address is on the [contact](../contatti/) page.
