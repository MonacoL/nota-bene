## The staff

Music is written on five parallel lines, the **staff** (British: stave). Notes sit on the lines or in the spaces between them: the higher a note is, the higher it sounds.

```abc
X:1
L:1/4
K:C
C D E F | G A B c |]
```

Above is a scale going up from C4 to C5. C4 is middle C on the keyboard, and it is written on a short ledger line below the staff.

## Whole steps and half steps

The **half step** (British: semitone) is the smallest distance between two notes: two neighboring keys on the keyboard, white or black. The **whole step** (British: tone) is two half steps. Between E4 and F4, and between B4 and C5, there is a half step; between the other neighboring white keys there is a whole step.

## Sharp, flat, and natural

The sharp (♯) raises a note by a half step, the flat (♭) lowers it by a half step, the natural (♮) cancels the accidental.

```abc
X:2
L:1/2
K:C
F ^F | B _B | =B2 |]
```
