## Number and quality

An **interval** is the distance between two notes. You find the number by counting the notes, the first and the last included: from C to E there are C, D, E, so it is a third. You find the quality by counting half steps: a major third spans 4, a minor third 3.

```abc
X:1
L:1/2
K:C
C E | E G | [CG] |]
```

## Inversion

In the inversion the lower note moves up an octave. The numbers add up to 9, major and minor swap, perfect stays perfect.
