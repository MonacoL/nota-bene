## The relative minor

Every key signature also belongs to a minor key, the **relative minor**: its tonic is a minor third below the major one. With no sharps or flats it is A minor.

## Three forms

The **natural** minor uses the notes of the key signature: W H W W H W W. The **harmonic** minor raises the 7th degree. The **melodic** minor, going up, raises the 6th and the 7th.
