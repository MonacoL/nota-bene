## Measures and beats

The **time signature** at the start of a piece tells you how many beats a measure (bar) has. In simple time (2/4, 3/4, 4/4) the beat is a quarter note, and eighth notes go in groups of 2.

## Compound time

In compound time (3/8, 6/8, 9/8, 12/8) the beat is a dotted quarter note, and eighth notes go in groups of 3. 3/4 and 6/8 last the same, but they are counted differently.

```abc
X:1
M:3/4
L:1/8
K:C
BB BB BB |]
```

```abc
X:2
M:6/8
L:1/8
K:C
BBB BBB |]
```
