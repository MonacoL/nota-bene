## How long a note lasts

The **note value** tells you how long a note lasts. Each one is worth half the one before: a whole note is worth two half notes, a half note two quarter notes (crotchets), a quarter note two eighth notes.

```abc
X:1
M:4/4
L:1/4
K:C
B4 | B2 B2 | B B B B | B/B/ B/B/ B/B/ B/B/ |]
```

## The dot

A dot after a note value adds half of its value: a dotted quarter note is worth three eighth notes.
