## Chromatic syllables

A note altered outside the key signature changes its vowel. Raised by a half step: DI RI FI SI LI. Lowered: RA ME SE LE TE.

```abc
X:1
L:1/4
K:C
C ^C D ^D | E F ^F G |]
w: DO DI RE RI MI FA FI SOL
```

## Do shift

When the music modulates, DO moves to the new tonic and the syllables change with it.
