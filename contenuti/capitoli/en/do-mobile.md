## The seven syllables

In **movable do** the tonic of a major key is sung DO, whatever note it is. The others follow the scale: DO RE MI FA SOL LA TI.

In D major, DO is D:

```abc
X:1
L:1/4
K:D
D E F G | A B c d |]
w: DO RE MI FA SOL LA TI DO
```

## Finding DO from the key signature

A key signature belongs to a major key and to its relative minor. In minor the tonic is sung LA, so the syllables stay the same: with 1 flat, DO is F, in F major and in D minor alike.

In the harmonic minor scale the raised leading tone is sung SI.
