## Whole and half steps in a row

The **major scale** goes up by whole steps (W) and half steps (H) in this order: W W H W W W H. Each letter appears only once.

```abc
X:1
L:1/4
K:C
D E ^F G | A B ^c d |]
```

## The key and its key signature

The scale's sharps or flats are written once, at the start of the staff: that is the **key signature**. With sharps the tonic is a half step above the last sharp; with flats it is the second-to-last flat.
